## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Category 6**. This trainer enhances Category 6 by reducing survival pressures and simplifying exploration, crafting, progression, inventory management, and encounters with aggressive wildlife and scavengers. It is designed for offline single-player use in both Story Mode and Survival Mode.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experience Category 6 with reduced survival pressure, faster crafting and progression, easier resource management, or more freedom to explore its storm-ravaged open world.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents damage from environmental hazards, hostile survivors, wildlife, and other threats.
- **Freeze Food, Water, and Shelter Needs** — Stops basic survival needs from decreasing during exploration.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Allows extended movement and survival activities without exhausting the character.
- **Instant Crafting** — Completes crafting actions immediately for faster preparation of tools, weapons, supplies, and improvised shelters.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Fast Movement** — Adjusts movement speed to make it easier to cross storm-damaged towns, forests, and other open-world areas.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Items** — Prevents selected consumables, tools, materials, and other carried items from being depleted.
- **Item Quantity Multiplier** — Increases the quantity gained from scavenging, gathering, and collecting valuable supplies.
- **Easy Trading** — Reduces the resource burden when trading valuable finds with the merchant.

### 📌 Progression

- **Experience Multiplier** — Increases experience gained from encounters and other activities to unlock character upgrades faster.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **One-Hit Defeat** — Greatly increases outgoing damage against hostile wildlife, scavengers, and other enemies.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate Fast Movement before crossing long distances or navigating difficult terrain. The feature is useful when traveling between abandoned locations, gathering areas, shelters, and merchant positions. Disable it when precise movement is needed around hazards, narrow paths, or interactive objects.

### Tools & Inventory

Use Unlimited Items and the Item Quantity Multiplier when collecting food, water, crafting materials, tools, weapons, and valuables. Category 6 automatically sorts carried items, so the trainer focuses on supply quantity rather than manual inventory organization. Easy Trading can help when exchanging valuable discoveries with the merchant.

### World & Environment

Story Mode follows a narrative journey through the hurricane's aftermath, while Survival Mode focuses on lasting as many days as possible. Use Freeze Needs to explore longer without constantly managing food, water, and shelter. Instant Crafting helps establish improvised shelters and prepare supplies before entering dangerous areas.

### Combat & Weapons

Category 6 features hostile wildlife, desperate scavengers, and other dangerous encounters. God Mode protects against incoming damage, while One-Hit Defeat shortens fights with hostile targets. Unlimited Items is useful for maintaining access to healing supplies and other consumables during extended expeditions.
